SAW Transducer Fabrication for EM Wave Modulation

Goal: Generate surface acoustic waves to modulate electromagnetic waves at a metal–dielectric interface.

Approach: Designed and fabricated IDTs using optimized lithography and thin‑film processes.

Tools: COMSOL, LTSpice, SEM, profilometry.

Outcome: Demonstrated controlled acoustic–electromagnetic coupling and characterized device response.

Thin‑Film Process Optimization

Improved thin‑film uniformity and adhesion through iterative DOE‑based parameter tuning. Reduced process variability and improved device yield across fabrication cycles.

Metrology Automation

Developed Python‑based scripts for automated data extraction and analysis from profilometry and SEM datasets, improving throughput and reducing manual processing time.
