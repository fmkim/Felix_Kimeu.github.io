Research Leadership

I lead semiconductor process optimization and surface science projects, coordinating experimental workflows and ensuring reproducibility across fabrication cycles. My work involves planning experiments, diagnosing process deviations, and implementing corrective actions.

Mentoring & Training

I mentor junior researchers and graduate students in cleanroom operations, lithography, metrology, and process troubleshooting. I also develop training documentation and SOPs to support safe and effective lab practices.

Cross‑Functional Collaboration

I work closely with faculty, technicians, and interdisciplinary teams to align experimental protocols, resolve equipment issues, and maintain cleanroom readiness. Collaboration is central to my approach to research and engineering.

Technical Communication

I deliver presentations in academic seminars, research meetings, and conference settings. My communication style emphasizes clarity, technical rigor, and visually engaging explanations of complex scientific concepts.
